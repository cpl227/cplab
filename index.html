About

The idea behind the lab

Years in mental health work will teach you one thing fast: knowing you need help and actually getting it are two very different timelines. Therapy helps. But waitlists are real, cost is real, and sometimes you're just not ready yet, and the hard part is, whatever you're feeling doesn't wait around for any of that to sort itself out.

"

This isn't anti-therapy.
It's what to do before it, in the meantime, or alongside it.

Every tool here is a self-help tool grounded in real, published research, cited by name throughout, not generic wellness advice dressed up to look official. Interactive and personalized. You answer, it decides, a next step built based on your answers.

This is a growing lab, not a finished product. New tools arrive as the collection grows, and the library above is where they all live.
